Our Products

A growing ecosystem of purposeful digital solutions.

01 — DignityPay

Financial Inclusion & Human Dignity

A digital payment and financial inclusion solution designed to help people in vulnerable situations access essential services and participate more easily in the digital economy.

02 — Debt Resolution

Connecting Creditors & Debtors

A digital platform designed to facilitate communication between creditors and debtors and help create possible paths toward resolving outstanding debts. (Note: Solviora does not provide legal or financial advisory services).

03 — Solviora Studio

Digital Creation Made Simpler

Digital tools and AI workflows designed to help individuals, creators and small businesses build websites, manage their digital presence and scale effectively.
